# Privacy Policy

**App:** Cabochons
**Effective date:** November 6, 2026

## The short version

Cabochons does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your data. Nothing leaves your device because of us.

## What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, the app:

- Does not collect personal information of any kind (no name, email, location, contacts, or identifiers).
- Does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- Does not create accounts or require a sign-in.
- Does not make network requests to our servers, because we don't run any.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter in the app, such as habits and progress, is stored locally on your device. We have no access to it. If you delete the app, that local data is removed with it.

If you have device-level backups enabled (for example, iCloud Backup), your device's backup may include the app's data. That is handled entirely by Apple under your own account and Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

## Third parties

We don't share data with third parties, because we don't have any data to share. The app contains no third-party services.

## Children's privacy

Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect none from children either.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever needs to collect data, this policy will be updated before that change ships.
